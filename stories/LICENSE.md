# Story licensing

Stories in this directory are original creative works and are **not** covered by
the repository's MIT license.

Every `stories/<slug>/` directory must contain a `LICENSE.md` naming the story's
copyright owner and terms. Unless that per-story file grants additional rights,
the story prose and story-specific creative assets are all rights reserved.

Do not add derivative works based on third-party fictional settings or copied
source material to this repository. Keep such experiments outside this Git
repository, even when they are private or noncommercial.
