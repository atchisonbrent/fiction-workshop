# Novella movements

## I. Residual heat

The refugee ship is gone. A routine reconciliation discovers that Lacuna Station
was warm for twelve minutes it cannot account for. Auditor Selen Marr initially
assumes theft, then realizes the discrepancy is both smaller and more dangerous:
somebody exercised moral judgment where the Directorate permits only allocation.

Near horizon: Selen interviews Nera without revealing what the ledger proves.
Nera refuses the easy lie that a sensor failed.

## II. The exemplary offender

Station factions compete to define Nera's act. Management wants an isolated
criminal; exhausted workers want a saint; families in the cold ring want to know
whose children lost heat. Selen discovers the Directorate's rules quietly depend
on undocumented exceptions elsewhere.

## III. A rule for mercy

Nera and Selen attempt to design a reviewable emergency protocol. Every safeguard
makes it slower; every shortcut recreates arbitrary authority. The Directorate
offers Selen autonomy in exchange for a public conviction.

## IV. Twelve minutes, distributed

The station must choose between compliance, revolt, and a protocol that openly
accepts measurable risk. Selen's choice resolves the audit. Nera pays a cost that
is neither execution nor effortless vindication. The ending preserves the saved
ship as a completed fact rather than converting it into a later rescue device.
