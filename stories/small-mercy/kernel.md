# Kernel: novella working revision

## Premise

After customs officer Nera Vale secretly grants a dying refugee ship twelve
minutes of illegal warmth, evidence of the act propagates through a station whose
survival depends on appearing perfectly obedient to a distant authority.

## Focal wants

- Nera wants to accept the personal cost without letting colleagues turn her act
  into either a martyrdom cult or an excuse for institutional violence.
- Auditor Selen Marr wants to prove the station can govern itself, but the cleanest
  proof would require sacrificing Nera to the Directorate.

## Story question

Can a humane exception become a durable rule without being converted into
propaganda, punishment, or permission for arbitrary power?

## Non-negotiable constraints inherited from `short-v1`

- The refugee ship departs before the short ends; it does not return to rescue Nera.
- Nera made the decision knowingly and alone.
- The twelve minutes saved lives but did not solve the refugees' larger danger.
- The short ending's emotional completion remains intact.

## Expansion vectors

Institutional memory, the cost of precedent, conflict between mercy and equal
process, and the people who must operate the station after the heroic moment.
