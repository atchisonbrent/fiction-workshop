# Expansion audit: `short-v1` to `novella-v1`

## Recommendation

Expand by **deepening + widening**, not by continuing with the refugee ship and
not by reframing the short's ending.

## Audit

1. **What made the short satisfying?** Nera faced one legible choice, made it
   without permission, saved strangers, and remained to bear the consequence.
2. **Promises that survive:** the rescue happened; her agency was real; mercy had
   a cost; the ship cannot solve the station's later problems.
3. **Compression-sensitive revelation:** the purpose of the stolen heat should
   remain late in the short. The novella may re-establish it early for new readers.
4. **Character pressure:** Nera can defend the choice but should resist becoming
   a symbol. Making her suddenly eager to lead a rebellion would be convenient.
5. **Immutable facts versus beliefs:** the diversion and departure are facts;
   Nera's belief that punishment will be simple is only a belief.
6. **New dramatic question:** can a humane exception become a durable,
   accountable rule?
7. **Standalone readers:** re-establish rationing, the twelve minutes, and the
   ship's inability to return without reproducing the full short.
8. **Why not sequel or no expansion?** The institutional consequences are latent
   inside the original choice and change its context without changing its result.
9. **Decision changed by this audit:** the first expansion pitch brought the
   refugee captain back to rescue Nera. Rejected. It would turn Nera's completed
   sacrifice into a transaction and make the short's ending provisional.
