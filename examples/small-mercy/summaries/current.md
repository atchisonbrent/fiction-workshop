# Current rolling summary

Lacuna Station, diverted twelve minutes of rationed heat to a refugee ship whose
passengers would otherwise have died before their drive restarted. She falsified
an automated refusal and stayed behind to accept discovery. The ship escaped;
there was no public reckoning inside the short.

The novella working set begins with the missing heat entering the audit ledger.

will deepen the cost of Nera's choice and widen the conflict into governance. It
must not revoke the rescue, return the refugees as saviors, or imply the short
was merely a prologue awaiting its real ending.
