# Novelty ledger

## Accepted mechanisms

- Institutional absurdity: a letter-only contamination code rejected because the field requires digits.
- Scale mismatch: a life-saving act appears as a twelve-minute ledger discrepancy.
- Ending texture: private moral completion followed by unresolved administrative consequence.

## Watch for repetition

- Do not solve the novella with another falsified form.
- Do not make every official secretly sympathetic or every rule obviously evil.
- Do not return the refugee ship as gratitude-powered cavalry.
- Limit jokes in official language; a mechanism becomes a tic quickly.
