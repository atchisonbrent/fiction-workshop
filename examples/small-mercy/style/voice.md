# Voice profile

- Close third person, initially Nera; Selen may become a second close POV in the novella.
- Clean sentences with occasional cumulative passages at moments of moral or physical scale.
- Exposition arrives through allocation, procedure, conflict, and concrete consequence.
- Humor comes from institutional language colliding with embodied need; it must not erase who pays.
- Emotional explicitness remains restrained. Choices and evasions carry the load.
- Moral posture: skeptical of unaccountable power without pretending every humane exception is safe.
- Avoid generic rebellion rhetoric, interchangeable quips, saintly refugees, and painless reform.
