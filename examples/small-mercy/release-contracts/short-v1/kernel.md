# Kernel: `short-v1`

A customs officer on a failing station receives a request for heat from a refugee
ship. The station cannot spare it without violating ration law and risking its
own cold compartments. She learns the request is not for comfort but to restart
a drive before enforcement arrives. She knowingly grants twelve minutes, saves
the strangers, falsifies the refusal, and stays to bear discovery.

The short promises one complete choice. It does not promise institutional reform
or reveal Nera's eventual punishment.
